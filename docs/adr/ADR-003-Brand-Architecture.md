# ADR-003 — Nexa Brand Architecture

Status

Accepted

Date

2026-08-05

---

## Context

The platform will expand beyond the beauty industry.

A brand strategy was required to support multiple business modules.

---

## Decision

Create a single master brand:

Nexa

Business segments become product modules.

Examples

- Nexa Beauty
- Nexa Sports
- Nexa Clinic
- Nexa Food
- Nexa Pet

The Nexa symbol remains the same across every module.

Only the module name and accent color may change.

---

## Alternatives

Independent brands.

Rejected.

Different logos for every industry.

Rejected.

Master brand with modules.

Accepted.

---

## Consequences

Positive

- Stronger brand recognition.
- Easier marketing.
- Consistent visual identity.

Negative

Requires careful brand governance.

---

## Future

Every new module must follow the same visual identity system.
