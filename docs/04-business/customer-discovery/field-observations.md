# Field Observation

Date: 2026-08-07

Interview Type:
Informal conversation

Business Type:
Independent Barber

Current Software:
Fresha

---

## Observations

- Uses Fresha as the primary scheduling system.
- Does not pay a monthly subscription.
- Pays only for WhatsApp automated messaging.
- Average monthly cost is around R$50.
- Monthly spending depends on message volume.
- Uses reminders, rescheduling notifications and cancellation messages.
- Works alone (single professional).
- Current pricing model is considered satisfactory by the user.

---

## Questions Raised

- How does Fresha monetize free users?
- What are the monthly message limits?
- Which features are unavailable in the free plan?
- Would this pricing model be sustainable for Nexa?
- Which value would justify paying a monthly subscription instead?

---

## Product Impact

None yet.

Requires validation with additional professionals.

---

## Hypothesis CD-001

Independent professionals are highly price-sensitive.

If a free scheduling platform solves most operational needs, they may not perceive enough value to migrate to a paid ERP.

This hypothesis must be validated with additional interviews.
