# Customer Discovery — Assumptions

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document records business assumptions identified during Customer Discovery.

Assumptions are hypotheses based on observations.

They are not considered facts until validated through multiple independent sources.

Every assumption should eventually become one of the following:

- Validated Learning
- Invalidated Assumption
- Product Decision (PDR)

---

# Review Process

Assumptions should be reviewed periodically.

Possible outcomes:

- Validated
- Invalidated
- Requires More Evidence

No assumption should remain indefinitely without review.

---

# Product Rule

Product decisions must never be based solely on assumptions.

Validated evidence should always precede Product Decisions (PDRs).
