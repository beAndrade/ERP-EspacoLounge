# Customer Discovery — Validated Learnings

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document records insights that have been validated through multiple independent observations.

A Validated Learning represents evidence rather than opinion.

Only validated learnings should influence Product Decisions (PDRs), Product Roadmaps and future EPICs.

---

# Validation Principles

A learning should only be considered validated when:

- Multiple businesses report the same problem.
- The pattern is observed consistently.
- Evidence comes from independent sources.
- The learning reflects real customer behavior.
- Product impact is clearly identified.

One interview is never enough.

# Review Process

Every validated learning should be reviewed periodically.

Possible outcomes:

- Still Valid
- Requires Revalidation
- Obsolete

Product evolution may invalidate previous learnings.

---

# Product Rule

Only validated learnings should influence:

- Product Roadmap
- PDRs
- EPIC prioritization
- Pricing Strategy
- Go-to-Market Strategy

Assumptions should never directly change the product.

---

# Success Criteria

Customer Discovery is considered effective when validated learnings consistently improve product decisions and reduce product assumptions over time.
