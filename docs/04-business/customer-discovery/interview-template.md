**Status:** Living Document

# Purpose

This template standardizes customer interviews conducted during the Customer Discovery process.

The objective is to understand how beauty businesses operate today, identify operational pain points and validate opportunities for the Nexa platform.

Interviews should collect evidence rather than confirm assumptions.
