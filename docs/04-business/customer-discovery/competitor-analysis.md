# Customer Discovery — Competitor Analysis

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document records competitive research conducted during Customer Discovery.

The objective is to understand how competing products solve customer problems, identify market trends and discover opportunities for differentiation.

Competitor analysis should guide strategic thinking rather than encourage feature copying.

---

# Analysis Principles

The goal is to understand:

- Which problems competitors solve.
- Which customer segments they target.
- Which business models they use.
- Which workflows they prioritize.
- Which opportunities remain underserved.

Nexa should compete through differentiation, not imitation.

# Review Process

Competitor analysis should be reviewed:

- Quarterly
- Before major Product EPICs
- Before pricing changes
- Before entering new market segments

---

# Product Rule

Competitor analysis should inspire questions, not product requirements.

Every product decision must still be validated through Customer Discovery and Product Decision Records (PDRs).

Competing by understanding customer problems is more sustainable than competing by copying features.
