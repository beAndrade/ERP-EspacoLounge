# Business Model

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the long-term business model of the Nexa platform.

It describes how Nexa creates value, delivers value and captures value while maintaining sustainable business growth.

The business model should evolve alongside customer needs and product maturity.

---

# Business Vision

Nexa exists to simplify the daily operation of beauty businesses.

Our goal is to become the operational platform that accompanies customers throughout every stage of their business growth.

Success is measured by customer success.

---

# Value Creation

Nexa creates value by helping beauty businesses:

- Organize daily operations
- Reduce administrative effort
- Improve customer management
- Standardize internal processes
- Increase operational visibility
- Make better business decisions

Operational excellence is our primary value proposition.

---

# Customer Segments

Primary

- Mega Hair Studios

Secondary

- Beauty Salons

Future

- Hair Clinics
- Beauty Franchises
- Other Beauty Segments

Expansion outside Beauty is not currently planned.

---

# Value Delivery

Value is delivered through:

- SaaS Platform
- Continuous Product Evolution
- Customer Success
- Product Education
- Operational Best Practices

Software alone is not enough.

The complete customer experience creates value.

---

Primary revenue:

Monthly SaaS subscription

Future revenue opportunities may expand beyond subscription (for example AI-related services, integrations, usage-based credits, and similar channels).

Recurring revenue supports continuous product evolution.

---

# Customer Relationship

Nexa seeks long-term partnerships rather than transactional sales.

Every customer interaction should strengthen trust and improve product understanding.

Customer relationships continue after onboarding through Customer Success and Product Discovery.

---

# Growth Model

Growth should occur through:

Satisfied Customers

↓

Customer Success

↓

Referrals

↓

Market Reputation

↓

New Customers

Organic trust is preferred over aggressive sales.

---

# Competitive Advantage

Nexa differentiates itself by:

- Deep specialization in Beauty
- Operational focus
- Continuous Customer Discovery
- Product evolution driven by evidence
- Long-term customer partnership

Competitive advantage should come from understanding customers better than competitors.

---

# Success Metrics

Business success should be evaluated through:

- Customer Retention
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Referral Rate
- Product Adoption

Financial growth should always follow customer success.

---

# Business Evolution

The business model should evolve gradually.

Every major change should be supported by:

- Customer Discovery
- Validated Learnings
- Product Decisions (PDRs)
- Business EPICs

The business model should remain simple, scalable and customer-centric.
