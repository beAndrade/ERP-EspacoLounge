# Positioning

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Nexa should be positioned in the market during its current stage of growth.

Positioning describes how customers should perceive Nexa and why they should choose it over alternative solutions.

This document should evolve alongside the product and the market.

---

# Current Positioning

Nexa is a modern operational platform designed to help businesses organize, simplify and evolve their daily operations.

The current commercial focus is the Beauty industry, beginning with Mega Hair specialists and beauty salons.

This market focus does not define the long-term identity of the platform.

---

# Positioning Principles

Nexa should be perceived as:

- Operational
- Modern
- Simple
- Reliable
- Intelligent
- Continuously evolving

The platform should reduce operational complexity rather than increase it.

---

# What Nexa Is

Nexa is:

- A business operations platform.
- A long-term operational partner.
- A continuously evolving SaaS platform.
- A product built around real customer problems.
- A platform that grows together with its customers.

---

# What Nexa Is Not

Nexa is not:

- A generic scheduling application.
- A collection of disconnected features.
- A product built from isolated customer requests.
- Software that customers eventually outgrow.

---

# Customer Promise

Nexa is committed to:

- Understanding customer operations.
- Solving real operational problems.
- Continuously improving the platform.
- Preserving simplicity as the product grows.
- Supporting customers throughout their business journey.

---

# Competitive Position

Nexa competes through:

- Customer understanding.
- Operational excellence.
- Continuous Product Discovery.
- Long-term partnerships.
- Sustainable product evolution.

Competitive advantage should come from better understanding customer operations rather than simply delivering more features.

---

# Communication Guidelines

When presenting Nexa:

Emphasize:

- Business outcomes.
- Operational efficiency.
- Simplicity.
- Customer partnership.
- Continuous evolution.

Avoid emphasizing:

- Number of features.
- Technical complexity.
- Technology stack.
- Feature comparisons.

Customers buy outcomes, not software specifications.

---

# Positioning Evolution

Current Position:

Operational platform focused on the Beauty industry.

Future Position:

A scalable operational platform capable of supporting multiple business domains while preserving the same customer-centric philosophy.

The market focus may evolve.

The platform philosophy should remain constant.
