# Go-To-Market Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Nexa enters the market, acquires its first customers and validates Product-Market Fit.

The initial objective is learning rather than rapid scale.

Every commercial action should increase customer understanding.

---

# Current Stage

Product Stage:

Pre Product-Market Fit

Business Stage:

Customer Validation

Commercial Strategy:

Founder-led Sales

---

# Initial Market

Primary Target

Mega Hair Studios

Secondary Target

Beauty Salons

Current geographic focus:

Local market

Expansion will occur only after successful customer validation.

---

# Initial Objectives

The first commercial phase aims to:

- Validate customer problems.
- Validate product value.
- Validate pricing.
- Validate onboarding.
- Build reference customers.
- Collect operational feedback.

Revenue is important.

Learning is essential.

---

# Customer Acquisition Strategy

Primary channels:

- Personal network
- In-person visits
- Referrals
- WhatsApp

Secondary channels:

- Instagram
- Landing Page
- Organic content

Paid acquisition is not a priority during this stage.

---

# Commercial Positioning

Every conversation should emphasize:

- Operational simplicity.
- Business organization.
- Continuous product evolution.
- Long-term partnership.

Avoid competing exclusively on features or price.

---

# Early Adopters

Ideal early customers:

- Open to technology.
- Willing to provide feedback.
- Operationally engaged.
- Interested in improving processes.

Early adopters become product partners.

---

# Product Validation Strategy

Validate:

- Scheduling
- Customer Management
- Financial Management
- Daily Operations
- Mobile Experience

Every validation should generate learning.

---

# Success Metrics

Measure:

- Customer interviews
- Product demonstrations
- Pilot customers
- Active customers
- Customer satisfaction
- Product adoption

The objective is to reach Product-Market Fit.

---

# Expansion Strategy

Expansion should occur gradually.

Current roadmap:

Local Market

↓

Regional Market

↓

National Market

↓

Additional Beauty Segments

↓

Future Business Domains

Every expansion should be supported by validated learnings.
