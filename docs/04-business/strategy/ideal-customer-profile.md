# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for the current stage of the Nexa platform.

The ICP represents the customer who is most likely to benefit from Nexa today.

It should evolve as the product matures and enters new markets.

---

# Current Product Stage

Product Stage:

Pre Product-Market Fit

Current objective:

Validate operational problems within the Beauty industry.

The ICP should remain focused while the product is still learning.

---

# Primary ICP

Business Type

Mega Hair Studios

---

Business Size

2–10 professionals

---

Business Stage

Growing business

Operational complexity increasing

Needs greater organization

---

Operational Characteristics

- Daily appointments
- Customer history
- Commission management
- Financial control
- Inventory management
- High customer retention

---

Technology Profile

Comfortable using digital tools.

Open to improving existing processes.

Not necessarily highly technical.

---

Buying Motivation

Primary motivations:

- Save time
- Organize operations
- Reduce mistakes
- Improve visibility
- Standardize workflows

---

Pain Points

Typical operational challenges include:

- Disorganized scheduling
- Customer information scattered across multiple tools
- Manual commission calculation
- Weak financial visibility
- Inventory control difficulties
- Heavy dependence on WhatsApp

These pain points should be validated continuously through Customer Discovery.

---

# Customers Outside the ICP

Examples:

- Independent professionals
- Very large franchise networks
- Businesses seeking only online booking
- Businesses unwilling to adopt digital processes

These segments may become relevant in the future but are not the current priority.

---

# ICP Evolution

The Ideal Customer Profile should evolve according to:

- Customer Discovery
- Validated Learnings
- Product-Market Fit
- Business Strategy

Future versions may include additional Beauty segments and, eventually, new business domains.

---

# Success Criteria

The ICP is considered effective when:

- Customer acquisition becomes more predictable.
- Sales conversations become easier.
- Product feedback becomes more consistent.
- Customer retention remains high.

---

# Relationship with Other Documents

This document works together with:

- Customer Discovery
- Sales Strategy
- Go-To-Market Strategy
- Pricing Strategy
- Product Roadmap

The ICP defines who Nexa should prioritize during its current stage of growth.

It should guide sales and product decisions without limiting future expansion.
