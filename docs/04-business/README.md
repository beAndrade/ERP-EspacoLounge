# Business Documentation

## Purpose

This directory contains the business strategy documentation for the Nexa platform.

Its purpose is to define how the product creates value, acquires customers, generates revenue and scales as a SaaS business.

Unlike Product documentation, this directory focuses on business execution rather than software implementation.

---

# Scope

Business documentation includes:

- Customer Discovery
- Market Validation
- Sales Strategy
- Pricing
- Customer Onboarding
- Customer Success
- Business Metrics
- Growth Strategy

The objective is to transform product capabilities into a sustainable business.

# Business Principles

Business decisions should prioritize:

- Customer Value
- Sustainable Growth
- Operational Simplicity
- Recurring Revenue
- Long-Term Relationships
- Continuous Customer Feedback

Every business decision should ultimately improve customer success.

---

# Business Workflow

Every business initiative should follow this sequence:

1. Discover
2. Validate
3. Define
4. Execute
5. Measure
6. Improve

Assumptions should always be validated before becoming product decisions.

# Customer Discovery

Customer Discovery is the foundation of every business decision.

The objective is to understand:

- Customer problems
- Current workflows
- Existing software
- Purchasing decisions
- Willingness to pay
- Product expectations

Validated learnings should be documented and shared with Product.

---

# Business Strategy

The current strategy is:

- Focus exclusively on the Beauty market.
- Become the reference ERP for Mega Hair specialists.
- Solve operational problems before expanding into new markets.
- Validate with real customers before scaling.

Expansion into other industries will only occur after achieving product-market fit within Beauty.

---

# Success Metrics

Business success should be measured through:

- Customer Acquisition
- Customer Retention
- Customer Satisfaction
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Churn Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Metrics should guide future business decisions.
