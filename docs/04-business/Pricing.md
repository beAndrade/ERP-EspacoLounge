# Pricing Strategy

## Purpose

Define the pricing strategy for the Nexa Platform.

Pricing should reflect value delivered rather than software features alone.

---

# Business Model

Software as a Service (SaaS)

Monthly subscription.

No perpetual licenses.

Automatic updates.

Cloud hosting included.

Support included.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Customers pay for business outcomes.

Not for software features.

Nexa sells:

- Organization
- Productivity
- Financial visibility
- Operational control
- Business growth

---

# Initial Plans

## Starter

Target

Small businesses

Includes

- Up to 5 users
- Core features
- Dashboard
- Scheduling
- Financial

---

## Professional

Target

Growing businesses

Includes

- More users
- Advanced reports
- Marketing
- Automation

---

## Enterprise

Target

Large businesses

Includes

- Unlimited users
- Priority support
- Custom modules
- API access

---

# Future Pricing Variables

- Number of users
- Active professionals
- Module enabled
- Storage
- Integrations

---

# Current Strategy

Focus on customer acquisition.

Validate pricing through real customers.

Optimize pricing after product-market fit.
