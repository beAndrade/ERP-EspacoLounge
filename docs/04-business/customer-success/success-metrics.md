# Customer Success Metrics

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Customer Success Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the key metrics used to evaluate customer success.

The objective is to measure whether customers are achieving meaningful business outcomes with Nexa.

Success should be measured by customer results rather than software usage alone.

---

# Customer Success Philosophy

Customer Success begins after the sale.

The objective is to help customers continuously improve their business operations.

Customer Success is responsible for maximizing:

- Product Adoption
- Customer Confidence
- Customer Retention
- Customer Growth
- Long-Term Partnership

---

# Customer Success Metrics Hierarchy

Customer Success metrics should be interpreted in the following order of importance.

Higher-level metrics represent stronger evidence of customer success than lower-level metrics.

The objective is to measure real customer outcomes rather than isolated satisfaction scores.

---

## Tier 1 — Value Realization

These metrics indicate whether customers are experiencing meaningful business value.

### Time to First Value (TTFV)

Definition

The time between customer purchase and the first moment of meaningful operational value.

Examples

- First successful appointment
- First payment recorded
- First commission calculated
- First completed daily workflow

Objective

Minimize Time to First Value while preserving customer confidence.

---

### Product Adoption

Definition

Percentage of core business workflows actively used by the customer.

Examples

- Scheduling
- Customer Management
- Financial Control
- Inventory
- Dashboard

Objective

Ensure customers adopt the workflows that generate business value.

---

## Tier 2 — Customer Retention

These metrics indicate whether customers continue generating value over time.

### Customer Retention

Measure

- Renewal Rate
- Active Subscriptions
- Churn Rate

Objective

Maintain long-term customer relationships.

---

### Customer Expansion

Measure

- Plan upgrades
- Additional users
- Optional services

Expansion should naturally result from customer success.

---

## Tier 3 — Customer Perception

These metrics help understand customer sentiment but should never replace behavioral metrics.

### Customer Satisfaction

Possible measurements

- NPS
- CSAT
- Customer Interviews
- Success Reviews

Customer perception complements behavioral evidence.

---

### Customer Advocacy

Measure

- Referrals
- Testimonials
- Case Studies

Satisfied customers should naturally become advocates for Nexa.

---

# Success Dashboard

Track monthly in the following priority:

Tier 1

- Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Product Adoption

Tier 2

- Customer Retention
- Churn Rate
- Expansion Revenue

Tier 3

- Customer Satisfaction (NPS/CSAT)
- Referral Rate
- Customer Testimonials

Behavioral metrics should always be analyzed before perception metrics.

---

# Review Process

Review customer success metrics:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- After major Product EPICs

Use insights to improve onboarding, product and customer experience.

---

# Customer Success Principle

Customer Success should measure customer behavior before customer opinion.

Positive opinions are valuable, but sustainable business growth depends on customers consistently achieving operational success with the platform.

Behavioral evidence should always take precedence over survey responses when evaluating the health of a customer relationship.
