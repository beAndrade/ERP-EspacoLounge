# Customer Retention Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Customer Success Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Nexa approaches long-term customer retention.

Customer retention is the result of continuously delivering operational value rather than preventing cancellations.

The objective is to build long-term partnerships where customers naturally choose to remain with Nexa.

---

# Retention Philosophy

Customers should remain because Nexa consistently improves their business.

Retention should never rely on:

- Contract lock-in
- Artificial barriers
- Aggressive discounts
- Cancellation friction

Long-term retention is earned through continuous value creation.

---

# Retention Drivers

Primary retention factors include:

- Fast Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Strong Product Adoption
- Operational Reliability
- Customer Confidence
- Continuous Product Evolution
- Responsive Customer Success

Retention should be driven by customer outcomes rather than contractual obligations.

---

# Early Warning Signals

Customer Success should proactively monitor:

- Declining platform usage
- Reduced Product Adoption
- Repeated operational difficulties
- Missed onboarding milestones
- Negative feedback
- Increased support dependency

Early identification allows proactive intervention.

---

# Retention Actions

When customer health declines:

1. Understand the root cause.
2. Contact the customer.
3. Review operational challenges.
4. Identify barriers to adoption.
5. Define an improvement plan.
6. Follow up until customer health improves.

Every intervention should focus on helping the customer succeed.

---

# Churn Analysis

Every cancellation should be documented.

Record:

- Cancellation reason
- Customer segment
- Time as customer
- Product usage
- Product Adoption
- Health Score
- Improvement opportunities

Every churn event should generate learning.

---

# Win-Back Strategy

Former customers should remain part of Nexa's learning process.

Possible actions:

- Exit interview
- Follow-up after improvements
- Product update notifications
- Re-engagement campaigns

The objective is to understand whether Nexa has evolved enough to solve previous limitations.

---

# Retention Metrics

Monitor:

- Customer Retention Rate
- Churn Rate
- Revenue Retention
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Product Adoption
- Customer Health Score

Behavioral metrics should always be prioritized over perception metrics.

---


Retention is the consequence of continuously improving customer outcomes.
