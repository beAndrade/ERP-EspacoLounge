# Customer Health Score

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Customer Success Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Nexa evaluates the overall health of each customer relationship.

The Customer Health Score is an operational indicator that combines customer behavior, product adoption and business engagement.

Its objective is to identify customers who may require additional support before problems occur.

---

# Philosophy

Customer health should be measured primarily through behavior rather than subjective perception.

A healthy customer consistently generates value from the platform.

The Health Score is designed to support proactive Customer Success, not reactive support.

---

# Health Score Components

The Customer Health Score is calculated using multiple indicators.

Current weighting (subject to future validation):

| Metric | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Product Adoption | 30% |
| Time to First Value (TTFV) | 20% |
| Platform Activity | 20% |
| Feature Utilization | 15% |
| Customer Satisfaction | 10% |
| Support Interaction | 5% |

Future versions may adjust these weights based on validated customer data.

---

# Health Levels

## Excellent

Score

90–100

Characteristics

- Daily platform usage
- High feature adoption
- Low support dependency
- Positive customer feedback

Recommended Action

Maintain relationship.

Identify expansion opportunities.

---

## Healthy

Score

70–89

Characteristics

- Regular usage
- Core workflows adopted
- Minor questions

Recommended Action

Continue regular Customer Success follow-up.

---

## Attention

Score

50–69

Characteristics

- Low product adoption
- Inconsistent usage
- Repeated support requests

Recommended Action

Schedule customer review.

Identify adoption barriers.

---

## Critical

Score

Below 50

Characteristics

- Low activity
- Poor adoption
- Risk of churn

Recommended Action

Immediate Customer Success intervention.

Review onboarding and customer objectives.

---

# Health Indicators

Monitor:

- Login frequency
- Weekly active usage
- Core workflow completion
- Feature adoption
- Support requests
- Customer feedback
- Renewal behavior

Behavioral indicators should always receive greater weight than opinion-based indicators.

---

# Health Review

Review customer health:

- Weekly (high-value customers)
- Monthly (all active customers)

Document significant changes.

---

# Customer Success Actions

Health Score should determine Customer Success priorities.

Customers with lower scores should receive proactive engagement before renewal periods.
