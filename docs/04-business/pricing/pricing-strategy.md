# Pricing Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the pricing philosophy and commercial positioning of the Nexa platform.

Pricing should reflect customer value rather than software features.

The objective is to create a sustainable pricing model that supports long-term customer relationships and business growth.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Customers should pay for value delivered.

Pricing should never be based solely on:

- Number of screens
- Number of features
- Development effort

Pricing should represent the operational impact generated by Nexa.

---

# Current Strategy

Current market focus:

- Beauty Industry

Primary niche:

- Mega Hair Studios

Secondary market:

- Beauty Salons

Pricing strategy will initially prioritize simplicity over optimization.

---

# Pricing Principles

Pricing should be:

- Simple
- Transparent
- Predictable
- Sustainable
- Easy to understand

Customers should immediately understand what they are paying for.

---

# Value Drivers

Customers purchase Nexa because it helps them:

- Save time
- Organize operations
- Improve customer management
- Reduce operational mistakes
- Improve financial visibility
- Standardize workflows

These benefits justify pricing.

---

# Pricing Criteria

Pricing decisions should consider:

- Business size
- Operational complexity
- Customer value
- Support effort
- Product maturity

Never copy competitor pricing.

---

# Pricing Validation

Every pricing decision should be validated through:

- Customer interviews
- Customer Discovery
- Lost opportunities
- Competitor analysis
- Customer willingness to pay

Pricing evolves with market understanding.

---

# Success Indicators

Pricing strategy is successful when:

- Customers understand the value proposition.
- Price is rarely the primary objection.
- Customer retention remains high.
- Revenue supports sustainable product evolution.

---

# Future Review

Pricing should be reviewed:

- After Product-Market Fit
- After major Product EPICs
- Before entering new market segments
- When customer behavior changes significantly

Pricing changes should be deliberate and evidence-based.

---

# Relationship with Other Documents

This strategy works together with:

- Monetization Strategy
- Packaging
- Sales Strategy
- Customer Discovery

Pricing should always support Nexa's long-term positioning rather than short-term sales.
