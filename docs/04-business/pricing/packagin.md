# Packaging Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how the Nexa platform is packaged and offered to customers.

Packaging determines how the product is presented commercially, how plans evolve over time and how additional value is introduced as the platform matures.

Packaging should simplify purchasing decisions rather than complicate them.

---

# Packaging Philosophy

Packaging should reflect customer maturity, not artificial feature restrictions.

Customers should clearly understand:

- What they receive
- Why it creates value
- How the product evolves with their business

The initial objective is simplicity.

---

# Current Packaging Strategy

Current Product Stage:

Pre Product-Market Fit

Current Offering:

One Product

One Plan

One Customer Experience

At this stage, all customers receive the same core product.

The priority is learning customer behavior before introducing commercial complexity.

---

# Why a Single Plan?

Current objectives:

- Validate Product-Market Fit
- Simplify commercial conversations
- Reduce implementation complexity
- Accelerate customer learning
- Avoid premature segmentation

The product should evolve before the pricing model evolves.

---

# Packaging Review

Packaging should be reviewed when:

- Product-Market Fit is achieved.
- Customer segments become clearly differentiated.
- Expansion opportunities emerge.

Packaging should evolve gradually.

# Packaging Philosophy Evolution

Nexa does not intend to differentiate plans primarily by limiting core product functionality.

As the platform matures, packaging should evolve according to the customer's business maturity rather than the number of available features.

The long-term vision is to support customers throughout their growth journey.

Illustrative progression:

|      Customer Stage      |      Potential Plan      |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Independent Professional |          Starter         |
|       Small Studio       |          Studio          |
|   Growing Beauty Salon   |         Business         |
| Multi-Location Operation |        Enterprise        |

These names are illustrative and do not represent finalized commercial offerings.

Future packaging decisions should be validated through:

- Customer Discovery
- Validated Learnings
- Product-Market Fit
- Customer Success Metrics

The objective is to ensure that customers naturally migrate to more advanced plans as their operational complexity increases, rather than because essential features become unavailable.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to create a flexible commercial model where customers naturally adopt additional capabilities as their business grows.

Nexa should grow together with its customers.

Commercial complexity should always remain lower than product value.
