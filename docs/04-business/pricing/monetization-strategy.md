# Monetization Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how the Nexa platform generates sustainable recurring revenue.

The objective is to create a business model aligned with customer success while supporting continuous product evolution.

Monetization should always reinforce customer value rather than restrict product adoption.

---

# Monetization Philosophy

Customers should pay because Nexa helps their business grow, not because essential functionality is artificially restricted.

Revenue should increase as customer success increases.

Long-term relationships are more valuable than short-term revenue.

---

# Primary Revenue Model

Current model:

Software as a Service (SaaS)

Revenue Source:

Monthly Subscription

Recurring Revenue:

Yes

Primary objective:

Build predictable Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

---

# Initial Strategy

During the Product Validation phase:

- One product
- One pricing model
- One customer experience

The priority is learning, not maximizing revenue.

---

# Future Revenue Opportunities

Potential future monetization channels:

- Premium Plans
- Additional Users
- Premium Integrations
- AI Features
- WhatsApp Credits
- Advanced Analytics
- White Label
- API Access
- Marketplace
- Training
- Consulting

Future monetization should only be introduced after clear customer validation.

---

# Pricing Alignment

Revenue should always remain aligned with:

- Customer value
- Product maturity
- Market positioning
- Operational simplicity

Avoid charging for features that customers perceive as essential.

---

# Customer Success Alignment

Successful customers should naturally generate sustainable recurring revenue.

The business should avoid monetization strategies that create friction or reduce product adoption.

---

# Business Metrics

Track:

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Churn
- Expansion Revenue

Metrics should guide monetization decisions.

---

# Expansion Strategy

Future monetization should prioritize:

Existing customer expansion

↓

Higher customer value

↓

Natural revenue growth

Rather than aggressive acquisition alone.

---

# Review Process

Review monetization strategy:

- After Product-Market Fit
- Before introducing new plans
- Before entering new markets
- After major Product EPICs

Monetization decisions should always support sustainable product growth.
