# Customer Onboarding Process

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the standard onboarding process for new Nexa customers.

The objective is to ensure every customer successfully adopts the platform with minimal friction and reaches their first operational success as quickly as possible.

A successful onboarding is measured by customer confidence rather than onboarding speed.

---

# Onboarding Philosophy

The onboarding process should reduce uncertainty.

Customers should never feel abandoned.

Every step should increase confidence and product understanding.

The objective is to guide customers toward operational independence.

---

# Onboarding Objectives

Every onboarding should achieve the following outcomes:

- Customer understands the platform.
- Business data is correctly configured.
- Team members know how to use Nexa.
- Daily operations can begin immediately.
- Customer reaches First Value quickly.

# Stage 01 — Welcome

Objective

Create a positive first impression.

Activities

- Welcome customer.
- Present onboarding roadmap.
- Explain expectations.
- Schedule onboarding sessions.

Customer Outcome

"I know what happens next."

---

# Stage 02 — Business Assessment

Objective

Understand how the customer's business operates.

Activities

- Professionals
- Services
- Pricing
- Commission rules
- Customer workflow
- Financial process

Customer Outcome

"They understand my business."

---

# Stage 03 — System Configuration

Objective

Configure Nexa according to the customer's operation.

Activities

- Company information
- Professionals
- Services
- Products
- Categories
- Financial settings

Customer Outcome

"The system reflects my business."

---

# Stage 04 — Data Migration

Objective

Transfer important information.

Possible sources

- Excel
- Google Sheets
- Existing software
- Manual records

Priority

```text
Customer data

↓

Services

↓

Products

↓

Historical information
```

Customer Outcome

"I don't have to start from zero."

---

# Stage 05 — Initial Training

Objective

Teach only what is necessary.

Topics

- Scheduling
- Customers
- Financial
- Daily routine

Avoid overwhelming customers with advanced functionality.

Customer Outcome

"I can already work with Nexa."

---

# Stage 06 — Go Live

Objective

Start real daily operation.

Activities

- First appointments
- First customer registration
- First payment
- First commission
- First dashboard review

Customer Outcome

"The platform is part of my daily routine."

---

# Stage 07 — First Value

Objective

Ensure the customer experiences meaningful operational improvement.

Examples

- Faster scheduling
- Better organization
- Less manual work
- Greater visibility

Customer Outcome

"Nexa is already helping my business."

---

# Stage 08 — Follow-up

Objective

Confirm successful adoption.

Topics

- Questions
- Difficulties
- Feedback
- Improvement opportunities

Customer Outcome

"I have support whenever I need it."

---

# Onboarding Principles

Always:

- Keep explanations simple.
- Prioritize daily workflows.
- Build confidence gradually.
- Encourage customer questions.
- Adapt to the customer's pace.

Never:

- Rush onboarding.
- Demonstrate every feature.
- Leave configuration incomplete.
- Assume customer understanding.

---

# Success Indicators

Measure:

- Time to First Value
- Onboarding Completion Rate
- Product Adoption
- Customer Satisfaction
- Support Requests
- Customer Confidence
