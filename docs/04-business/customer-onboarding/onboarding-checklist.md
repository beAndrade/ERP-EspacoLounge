# Customer Onboarding Checklist

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This checklist standardizes the onboarding execution process for every new Nexa customer.

Its objective is to ensure consistency, reduce onboarding errors and verify that every customer is prepared for daily operation before Go Live.

The checklist should be completed for every customer.
