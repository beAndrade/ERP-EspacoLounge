# Customer Journey

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end journey of a Nexa customer.

The objective is to understand every customer interaction from the first contact to long-term partnership.

Each stage should deliver value while strengthening customer confidence in the platform.

---

# Journey Philosophy

Customers do not simply purchase software.

They begin a long-term relationship with Nexa.

Every interaction should increase:

- Trust
- Product Adoption
- Operational Efficiency
- Customer Success

# Stage 01 — Prospect

Customer becomes aware of Nexa.

Goal:

Generate curiosity.

Customer Question:

"Could this help my business?"

---

# Stage 02 — Discovery

Understand the customer's operation.

Goal:

Identify real business problems.

Customer Question:

"Do they understand my business?"

---

# Stage 03 — Product Demonstration

Show how Nexa solves those problems.

Goal:

Generate confidence.

Customer Question:

"Can this really improve my daily work?"

---

# Stage 04 — Proposal

Present commercial conditions.

Goal:

Reduce uncertainty.

Customer Question:

"Is this worth the investment?"

---

# Stage 05 — Purchase

Customer decides to move forward.

Goal:

Create excitement.

Customer Question:

"Did I make the right decision?"

---

# Stage 06 — Onboarding

Configure Nexa for the customer's business.

Goal:

Reduce friction.

Customer Question:

"How quickly can I start using it?"

---

# Stage 07 — First Value

Customer successfully completes important daily tasks.

Examples:

- Schedule appointments
- Register customers
- Record payments

Goal:

Deliver the first meaningful success.

Customer Question:

"This is already making my life easier."

---

# Stage 08 — Daily Usage

Nexa becomes part of the customer's routine.

Goal:

Build habits.

Customer Question:

"I can't imagine working without this."

---

# Stage 09 — Customer Success

Customer continuously improves operational efficiency.

Goal:

Increase long-term value.

Customer Question:

"What else can Nexa help me improve?"

---

# Stage 10 — Advocate

Satisfied customers naturally recommend Nexa.

Goal:

Generate referrals.

Customer Question:

"I should recommend this to other businesses."
