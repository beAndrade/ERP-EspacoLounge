# Business Decision Framework

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the principles that guide strategic business decisions at Nexa.

Its objective is to ensure that commercial, product and operational decisions remain consistent as the company grows.

Business decisions should be based on evidence rather than assumptions.

---

# Core Philosophy

Nexa exists to solve real operational problems.

Every business decision should increase customer value while preserving long-term sustainability.

Short-term opportunities should never compromise long-term vision.

---

# Decision Hierarchy

Business decisions should follow this sequence:

```text
Idea
        │
        ▼
Customer Discovery
        │
        ▼
Field Observations
        │
        ▼
Assumptions
        │
        ▼
Validated Learnings
        │
        ▼
Business Decision
        │
        ▼
Product Decision (PDR)
        │
        ▼
Product EPIC
        │
        ▼
Implementation
```

Ideas alone should never become product features.

---

# Decision Principles

## Principle 01 — Evidence Before Opinion

Business decisions should rely on validated evidence.

Individual opinions, isolated customer requests or internal preferences should never be sufficient to justify strategic changes.

---

## Principle 02 — Customer Problems Before Features

Customers do not purchase features.

Customers purchase better business outcomes.

Every discussion should begin with the customer's operational problems.

---

## Principle 03 — Learn Before Scaling

The objective during the current stage is learning.

Scaling should occur only after Product-Market Fit has been validated.

---

## Principle 04 — Simplicity First

Prefer simple solutions whenever they solve the customer's problem effectively.

Complexity should only be introduced when justified by validated business value.

---

## Principle 05 — Long-Term Thinking

Avoid decisions that generate short-term gains while reducing long-term customer trust.

Customer relationships are more valuable than immediate revenue.

---

## Principle 06 — Customer Success Drives Business Success

Revenue is the consequence of customer success.

Improving customer outcomes should always be prioritized over increasing short-term sales.

---

## Principle 07 — Continuous Learning

Every customer interaction should generate knowledge.

Sales, onboarding, support and Customer Success all contribute to Product Discovery.

Learning is part of every department.

---

# Business Rules

The following actions require evidence before execution:

- Major pricing changes.
- Expansion into new market segments.
- New commercial plans.
- New monetization strategies.
- Significant positioning changes.
- Large product investments.

Whenever possible, decisions should be supported by Customer Discovery.

---

# Decision Criteria

Before approving any significant business decision, ask:

1. Which customer problem are we solving?

2. What evidence supports this decision?

3. Which customer segment benefits?

4. Does this align with Nexa's long-term vision?

5. Does this increase operational simplicity?

6. Can this decision be validated?

7. How will success be measured?

If these questions cannot be answered confidently, additional discovery may be required.

---

# Current Business Priorities

Current priorities are:

1. Validate Product-Market Fit.

2. Build strong customer relationships.

3. Learn continuously from real customers.

4. Improve operational excellence.

5. Create sustainable recurring revenue.

Growth should follow learning.

---

# Decision Anti-Patterns

Avoid decisions based on:

- A single customer request.
- Competitor imitation.
- Personal preference.
- Technology trends.
- Feature quantity.
- Short-term revenue only.

Business decisions should remain customer-centric.

---

# Continuous Improvement

This framework should evolve as Nexa grows.

Business principles may be refined through:

- Customer Discovery
- Product Evolution
- Business EPICs
- Company experience

The framework itself should remain simple, practical and actionable.

Nexa should never optimize for software alone.

Every decision should strengthen the relationship between customer success, product evolution and sustainable business growth.

The platform should evolve because customers evolve.

That principle should remain valid regardless of market, product or company size.
