# Objection Handling

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines how common sales objections should be handled during the commercial process.

The objective is not to convince customers at any cost.

The objective is to understand the concern behind every objection and determine whether Nexa is the right solution.

Every objection is valuable customer feedback.

---

# Principles

When an objection appears:

1. Listen completely.
2. Clarify the concern.
3. Understand the underlying problem.
4. Respond honestly.
5. Never promise unavailable features.
6. Record recurring objections.

Objections should improve both the product and the sales process.

# Common Objections

---

## "I already use another system."

### Concern

Customer does not perceive enough value to change.

### Response

Understand what works well today.

Identify operational problems that remain unresolved.

Avoid criticizing competitors.

---

## "The price is too high."

### Concern

Customer does not yet perceive sufficient value.

### Response

Return to operational problems.

Demonstrate business impact rather than software features.

Avoid discounting immediately.

---

## "I don't have time to learn a new system."

### Concern

Fear of operational disruption.

### Response

Explain the onboarding process.

Highlight gradual adoption.

Demonstrate simplicity.

---

## "My current process works."

### Concern

Customer does not perceive current inefficiencies.

### Response

Ask operational questions.

Help the customer identify hidden costs.

Never argue.

---

## "I'll think about it."

### Concern

Usually indicates unresolved uncertainty.

### Response

Ask:

"What would you like to better understand before making a decision?"

Avoid applying pressure.

---

# Objection Log

Every recurring objection should be documented.

Record:

- Date
- Customer Type
- Context
- Resolution
- Outcome

Patterns should influence Product and Sales Strategy.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Review objections regularly.

Questions:

Are new objections appearing?

Are old objections disappearing?

Which objections occur most frequently?

Can Product solve them?

Can Sales communicate value better?
