# Sales Conversation Library

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document captures valuable insights from real sales conversations.

The objective is to preserve knowledge gained during customer interactions and continuously improve Nexa's commercial process.

Every meaningful conversation should contribute to the evolution of Sales, Product and Customer Discovery.

---

# Principles

This is not a CRM.

It is a knowledge base.

Record conversations that generated important learnings, revealed recurring customer behavior or improved the sales process.

Focus on patterns rather than isolated events.

# Conversation Log

Record every meaningful commercial interaction using the template above.

Over time, recurring patterns should emerge.

---

# Monthly Review

At the end of each month review:

- Most common pain points
- Most frequent objections
- Competitors mentioned
- Features generating the most interest
- Lost opportunities
- Closed customers

Document recurring patterns.

---

# Relationship with Other Documents

Insights collected here should feed:

- Customer Discovery
- Field Observations
- Assumptions
- Validated Learnings
- Competitor Analysis
- Sales Strategy
- Product Decision Records (PDRs)

This document connects daily customer conversations with long-term product evolution.

---

# Success Criteria

The library is successful when:

- Sales conversations become more consistent.
- Customer patterns become easier to identify.
- Product decisions rely on real evidence.
- Commercial knowledge is preserved over time.

Knowledge collected here should compound with every customer interaction.
