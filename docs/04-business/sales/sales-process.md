# Purpose

This document defines the official sales process for the Nexa platform.

The objective is to create a repeatable and measurable commercial process that transforms qualified leads into successful customers.

Every opportunity should follow the same structured journey.

# Sales Philosophy

The sales process should prioritize:

- Understanding customer operations
- Identifying real business problems
- Building trust
- Demonstrating value
- Validating product fit

The objective is not to close sales quickly.

The objective is to build long-term customer relationships.

# Stage 01 — Prospect

## Goal

Identify potential customers.

Examples:

- Mega Hair Studios
- Beauty Salons
- Hair Clinics

---

## Activities

- Research business
- Collect contact information
- Understand business profile

---

## Exit Criteria

Potential customer identified.

---

# Stage 02 — First Contact

## Goal

Schedule an introductory conversation.

---

## Activities

- Present yourself
- Explain purpose
- Schedule discovery meeting

Avoid presenting the product immediately.

---

## Exit Criteria

Discovery meeting scheduled.

---

# Stage 03 — Discovery

## Goal

Understand the customer's operation.

---

## Activities

- Ask operational questions
- Identify pain points
- Understand current software
- Observe workflow

Reference:

Customer Discovery Interview Template.

---

## Exit Criteria

Customer problems clearly understood.

---

# Stage 04 — Qualification

## Goal

Determine whether Nexa is a good fit.

---

## Qualification Criteria

- Beauty business
- Operational complexity
- Interest in improving processes
- Willingness to adopt software

---

## Exit Criteria

Qualified or disqualified.

---

# Stage 05 — Product Demonstration

## Goal

Demonstrate how Nexa solves the customer's problems.

---

## Demonstration Principles

Show workflows.

Avoid feature lists.

Focus on:

- Scheduling
- Customer Management
- Financial Control
- Daily Operations

---

## Exit Criteria

Customer understands product value.

---

# Stage 06 — Proposal

## Goal

Present commercial conditions.

---

## Activities

- Pricing
- Plan
- Implementation timeline
- Next steps

---

## Exit Criteria

Proposal delivered.

---

# Stage 07 — Pilot (Optional)

## Goal

Validate product fit in a real environment.

---

## Activities

- Configure system
- Follow customer usage
- Collect feedback

---

## Exit Criteria

Pilot completed.

---

# Stage 08 — Customer

## Goal

Formalize commercial relationship.

---

## Activities

- Contract
- Account creation
- Initial configuration

---

## Exit Criteria

Customer officially onboarded.

# Success Criteria

The sales process is successful when:

- Every opportunity follows the same flow.
- Conversion rates become measurable.
- Customer expectations remain aligned.
- Onboarding becomes predictable.

# Continuous Improvement

Review the sales process regularly.

Use:

- Customer feedback
- Sales metrics
- Lost opportunities
- Customer Discovery learnings

The process should evolve continuously without losing consistency.

Product improvements may originate from:

- Customer interviews
- Support requests
- Customer Success meetings
- Product usage observation
- Lost sales
- Customer onboarding
- Competitor analysis

# Product Rule

Individual customer requests should never become immediate product features.

Every request should pass through the following validation process:

1. Register the observation.
2. Create or update an Assumption.
3. Validate with additional customers.
4. Record a Validated Learning.
5. Create or update a Product Decision Record (PDR).
6. Prioritize within the Product Roadmap.
7. Implement through the appropriate Product EPIC.

This process ensures that Nexa evolves based on validated market needs rather than isolated feature requests.
