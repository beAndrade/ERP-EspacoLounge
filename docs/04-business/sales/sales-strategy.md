# Sales Strategy

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the commercial strategy for the Nexa platform.

Its purpose is to establish how Nexa acquires customers, positions itself in the Beauty market and scales its commercial operation.

Sales should be driven by customer problems rather than product features.

---

# Sales Philosophy

Nexa does not sell software.

Nexa sells operational efficiency.

The product should solve real business problems before introducing additional functionality.

Every sales conversation should begin with the customer's daily operation.

---

# Current Business Strategy

Current market focus:

- Beauty Industry

Primary niche:

- Mega Hair Studios

Secondary targets:

- Beauty Salons
- Hair Studios

Not in scope:

- Independent professionals
- Barbershops
- General scheduling businesses

Expansion to other markets will occur only after achieving Product-Market Fit.

---

# Sales Principles

Every sales interaction should:

- Understand before presenting.
- Listen more than speak.
- Demonstrate value through real workflows.
- Focus on business outcomes.
- Avoid technical discussions unless requested.

---

# Value Proposition

Nexa helps beauty businesses:

- Reduce operational effort.
- Organize customer management.
- Simplify financial control.
- Standardize internal processes.
- Increase operational visibility.
- Improve business decision-making.

Scheduling is only one part of the solution.

# Sales Approach

Focus conversations on:

Current operation

↓

Daily problems

↓

Current software

↓

Operational inefficiencies

↓

Business impact

↓

How Nexa improves the workflow

Avoid feature-by-feature demonstrations.

---

# Sales Goals

Short Term

- Validate Product-Market Fit.
- Learn customer language.
- Understand objections.
- Build first customer base.

Medium Term

- Develop repeatable sales process.
- Create referral engine.
- Standardize onboarding.

Long Term

- Build scalable SaaS acquisition process.

---

# Success Indicators

Sales strategy is working when:

- Customer conversations become predictable.
- Objections repeat consistently.
- Product demonstrations follow the same flow.
- Customer acquisition becomes repeatable.
- Referrals increase naturally.

---

# Relationship with Product

Sales should continuously feed:

- Customer Discovery
- Product Decisions (PDRs)
- Product Roadmap

Sales should never dictate product direction based on isolated requests.
