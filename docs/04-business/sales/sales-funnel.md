# Sales Funnel

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the commercial funnel used by the Nexa platform.

The objective is to measure customer acquisition efficiency and continuously improve the sales process.

The funnel should prioritize quality over volume.

---

# Funnel Philosophy

Every stage should answer one question:

Can this opportunity realistically become a successful customer?

The objective is not to maximize leads.

The objective is to maximize successful long-term customers.

---

```text
Prospects
```

# Stage Definitions

## Prospect

Potential customer identified.

Success Metric:

Number of new prospects.

---

## First Contact

Initial conversation established.

Success Metric:

Contact Rate.

---

## Discovery

Customer needs understood.

Success Metric:

Discovery Meeting Rate.

---

## Qualified

Customer profile matches Nexa.

Success Metric:

Qualification Rate.

---

## Product Demonstration

Customer understands product value.

Success Metric:

Demo Completion Rate.

---

## Proposal

Commercial proposal delivered.

Success Metric:

Proposal Acceptance Rate.

---

## Negotiation

Commercial discussions underway.

Success Metric:

Negotiation Conversion Rate.

---

## Won

Customer accepted proposal.

Success Metric:

Close Rate.

---

## Onboarding

Customer begins implementation.

Success Metric:

Onboarding Completion Rate.

---

## Active Customer

Customer successfully uses Nexa.

Success Metric:

Activation Rate.

---

# Lost Opportunities

Every lost opportunity should record:

- Reason
- Competitor
- Pricing
- Product Gap
- Timing
- Other

No opportunity should be lost without learning.

---

# Funnel Metrics

Track:

- Total Prospects
- Contact Rate
- Discovery Rate
- Qualification Rate
- Demo Rate
- Proposal Rate
- Win Rate
- Activation Rate

---

# Funnel Review

Review monthly:

- Bottlenecks
- Conversion Rates
- Common objections
- Lost opportunity reasons
- Sales cycle duration

---

# Continuous Improvement

Every funnel metric should generate questions.

Example:

Low qualification rate?

↓

Improve prospecting.

Low demo conversion?

↓

Improve demonstrations.

Low activation?

↓

Improve onboarding.

Metrics should always lead to action.
