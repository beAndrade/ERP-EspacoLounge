# Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the primary customer personas for the Nexa platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that every product decision solves a real business problem.

Every new feature should answer:

- Which persona benefits from this?
- Which problem is being solved?
- Does this create real business value?

---

# 🥇 Primary Persona — Beauty Salon Owner

## Profile

Owns a beauty salon or hair studio.

Usually manages between 2 and 4 professionals.

Responsible for financial decisions.

Has little time to learn complex software.

She is a layperson when it comes to technology.

Usually manages the business through a mobile phone.

---

## Goals

- Organize appointments.
- Reduce scheduling mistakes.
- Control commissions.
- Track financial results.
- Improve customer experience.
- Save time.

---

## Pain Points

- Paper schedules.
- WhatsApp conversations mixed with appointments.
- Difficult commission calculations.
- Poor financial visibility.
- Lack of reports.
- Manual processes.

---

## What Nexa Solves

✓ Smart Scheduling

✓ Financial Dashboard

✓ Commission Management

✓ Customer History

✓ Inventory Control

✓ Reports

# Product Principles

Every feature developed for Nexa should improve the daily workflow of at least one persona.

Features that do not clearly benefit any persona should be reconsidered.

The product should prioritize simplicity over feature quantity.

The platform exists to save time, reduce operational complexity and improve business decision-making.
