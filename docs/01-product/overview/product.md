# Product

## What is Nexa?

Nexa is a cloud-based business management platform (SaaS) designed for small and medium-sized service companies.

The platform combines operational management, scheduling, financial control, customer relationship management and business intelligence in a single system.

---

## Target Audience

Primary

- Beauty salons
- Hair specialists
- Barbershops
- Beauty clinics

Future

- Sports arenas
- Gyms
- Medical clinics
- Restaurants
- Pet shops
- Service providers

---

## Main Goals

- Reduce operational complexity.
- Centralize business information.
- Improve financial control.
- Increase productivity.
- Deliver actionable business insights.

## Product Philosophy

The platform should feel modern, lightweight and easy to learn.

Technology should simplify work instead of creating complexity.
