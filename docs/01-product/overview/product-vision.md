# Nexa Vision

## Mission

Empower small and medium-sized service businesses with modern, intuitive and scalable management software that simplifies daily operations and supports business growth.

---

## Vision

Become the leading modular management platform for service businesses in Brazil, offering specialized solutions built on a single, scalable core platform.

---

## Core Values

### Simplicity

Complex business processes should feel simple to use.

### Reliability

The platform must be stable, predictable and trustworthy.

### Scalability

Every architectural decision should allow Nexa to grow into new industries without requiring major rewrites.

### User Experience

Every screen should be intuitive and require minimal training.

### Continuous Improvement

Real customer feedback drives product evolution.

---

## Long-term Vision

Nexa is not an ERP for beauty salons.

Nexa is a modular SaaS platform capable of serving multiple business segments through a shared Core Platform.

Current Module:

- Nexa Beauty

Future Modules:

- Nexa Sports
- Nexa Clinic
- Nexa Food
- Nexa Pet
- Nexa Academy

Every module shares the same Core while implementing its own business rules.
