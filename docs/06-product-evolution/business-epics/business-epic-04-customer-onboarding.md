# BUSINESS-EPIC-04 — Customer Onboarding

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Take a signed customer from contract to first week of real use with minimal risk, clear migration steps, and fast time-to-value.

## Why it matters

“I don’t have time to migrate” is a top objection. Onboarding quality decides whether Beauty excellence is felt.

## Outcomes

- Standard onboarding checklist (data, users, WhatsApp, training)
- Spreadsheet/XLSX import path documented and rehearsed
- Go-live criteria shared with customer before start
- First-value moment defined (e.g. first agenda day + first comanda paid)

## In scope

- Playbook for migration from Excel / previous system
- Role-based training outline (owner, receptionist, professionals)
- Configuration checklist (services, packages, commissions, stock)
- Pairing with product import tools (`docs/01-product/overview/spreadsheet-import.md`, seed lessons)

## Out of scope

- Fully self-serve signup for multi-tenant (depends on EPIC-04)
- Custom software development per customer as default
- Unlimited free parallel-run support without timeline

## Dependencies

- BUSINESS-EPIC-02 / 03
- Product import and Beauty catalog readiness (EPIC-03)
- `docs/03-development/engeneering/go-live-checklist.md`
- `docs/01-product/overview/spreadsheet-import.md`

## Success metrics

- Time-to-first-value (contract → first productive day)
- % of onboardings completed without emergency founder firefighting
- Customer confidence score at go-live (simple survey)
