# BUSINESS-EPIC-03 — Pricing Strategy

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Validate willingness to pay for Nexa Beauty outcomes (organization, productivity, financial visibility) and lock an initial packaging model.

## Why it matters

Pricing drafts exist (`docs/04-business/pricing/`). They must be pressure-tested with real buyers before SaaS scale.

## Outcomes

- Published starter packaging (seats, modules, implementation fee)
- Documented implementation vs subscription split
- Objection responses for “too expensive” grounded in value
- Decision log for discounts and exceptions

## In scope

- Price interviews and proposal experiments
- Align packaging with product reality (what is truly included today)
- Define implementation scope boundaries (migration from Excel/XLSX)
- Update Pricing / Monetization docs from evidence

## Out of scope

- Complex enterprise contracting for multi-brand chains (until EPIC-04)
- Usage-based AI pricing (EPIC-06 later)
- Competing as the cheapest tool on the market

## Dependencies

- BUSINESS-EPIC-01 / 02
- `docs/04-business/pricing/pricing-strategy.md`
- `docs/04-business/pricing/monetization-strategy.md`
- Clarity on onboarding cost (BUSINESS-EPIC-04)

## Success metrics

- % of proposals accepted at list price (or planned discount band)
- Implementation fee acceptance rate
- Churn attributable to price vs value mismatch
