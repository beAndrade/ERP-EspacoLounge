# BUSINESS-EPIC-02 — Sales Foundation

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Make founder-led sales repeatable: qualification → demo → proposal → close, with a living playbook.

## Why it matters

Nexa sells transformation, not feature lists. Without a repeatable motion, growth depends on luck.

## Outcomes

- Clear ICP and disqualify rules
- Standard demo flow driven by discovered pains
- Objection handling scripts tested in live calls
- Closing checklist before proposal / onboarding / go-live

## In scope

- Complete and maintain `docs/07-operations/playbooks/sales-playbook.md`
- Demo environments and scripts aligned to Beauty excellence moments
- Proposal template outline (commercial terms still under Pricing epic)
- Pipeline tracking (lightweight: spreadsheet or simple CRM)

## Out of scope

- Hiring a full sales team too early
- Feature-dump demos
- Discounting as primary strategy

## Dependencies

- BUSINESS-EPIC-01 evidence
- Product demo stability (EPIC-01 / EPIC-03 slices help)
- `docs/04-business/sales/sales-strategy.md`

## Success metrics

- Demo → proposal conversion rate
- Proposal → close conversion rate
- Average sales cycle length
- Playbook sections filled with real responses (not placeholders)
