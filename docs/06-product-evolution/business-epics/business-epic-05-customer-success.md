# BUSINESS-EPIC-05 — Customer Success

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Keep customers active, healthy and expanding — and turn successful salons into referrals.

## Why it matters

Founder-led growth in a local corridor depends on reputation. Success is the retention and expansion engine.

## Outcomes

- Health checks at 7 / 30 / 90 days
- Playbook for adoption gaps (agenda unused, Excel shadow returns)
- Expansion triggers (extra seats, WhatsApp depth, future modules)
- Referral ask built into success milestones

## In scope

- Define leading usage indicators (agenda events, comandas, payments)
- Cadence of check-ins for early customers
- Escalation path when churn risk appears
- Feed product backlog with recurring friction (links to product epics)
- Align with `docs/04-business/customer-success/success-metrics.md`

## Out of scope

- Enterprise CS org before volume justifies it
- Punitive lock-in tactics
- Promising roadmap features as support compensation

## Dependencies

- BUSINESS-EPIC-04 completed onboardings
- Product stability on critical paths (EPIC-01–03)
- Metrics definition in `docs/04-business/customer-success/success-metrics.md`

## Success metrics

- 30/90-day active usage
- Logo retention
- Referral-sourced pipeline
- Net expansion (seats / add-ons) when packaged
