# Business Roadmap

## Purpose

Sequence go-to-market and customer lifecycle work so product epics convert into revenue and retention.

Product delivery sequencing lives in [product-roadmap.md](./product-roadmap.md).

---

## Current baseline

- Founder-led sales (see `docs/04-business/sales/sales-strategy.md`)
- Geographic focus starts with Região dos Lagos corridor (Rio das Ostras → Macaé → Cabo Frio)
- Pricing and monetization drafts exist (`docs/04-business/pricing/`)
- Sales playbook scaffold exists (`docs/07-operations/playbooks/sales-playbook.md`) — needs real interview evidence

---

## Themes (ordered)

| Order | Theme | Primary epic | Intent |
|-------|-------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | Customer Discovery | BUSINESS-EPIC-01 | Learn pains from real salons |
| 2 | Sales Foundation | BUSINESS-EPIC-02 | Repeatable demo → close motion |
| 3 | Pricing Strategy | BUSINESS-EPIC-03 | Validate willingness to pay |
| 4 | Customer Onboarding | BUSINESS-EPIC-04 | Fast, low-risk migration to value |
| 5 | Customer Success | BUSINESS-EPIC-05 | Retain, expand, generate referrals |

Discovery should **lead** heavy product bets. Pricing validation should **precede** aggressive multi-tenant scale spend.

---

## Operating model

```text
Discovery interviews
        ↓
Sales playbook updates
        ↓
Pricing experiments
        ↓
Onboarding playbooks
        ↓
Success metrics + expansion
```

Each loop feeds Product Roadmap priorities (especially Beauty Excellence and Smart Booking).

---

## Geographic phases (from sales strategy)

1. Rio das Ostras / Macaé / Cabo Frio
2. Região dos Lagos
3. Rio de Janeiro
4. Brazil

Do not expand geography faster than onboarding and success capacity.

---

## Success signals

- Qualified conversations per week
- Demo → proposal conversion
- Time-to-first-value after contract
- 30/90-day retention / active usage
- Referral rate

Detailed metrics: `docs/04-business/customer-success/success-metrics.md`

---

## Non-goals

- Hiring a full sales team before founder-led motion is repeatable
- Competing on “cheapest ERP” positioning
- Promising multi-module platforms before Beauty excellence and SaaS runtime are credible
