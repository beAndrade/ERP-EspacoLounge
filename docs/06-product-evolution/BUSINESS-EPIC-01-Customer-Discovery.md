# BUSINESS-EPIC-01 — Customer Discovery

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Learn real salon pains, workflows and switching barriers through structured interviews — evidence before big product bets.

## Why it matters

Sales Playbook and competitive matrix must be based on customer feedback, not assumptions.

## Outcomes

- Interview script and notes template in active use
- Ranked pain list feeding Product Roadmap (especially EPIC-01–03)
- Updated Ideal Customer Profile (who to sell / who not to sell)

## In scope

- Founder-led interviews in target geography
- Capture system-in-use, likes, pains, shadow Excel/WhatsApp processes
- Feed `Sales-Playbook.md` Lessons Learned and Competitive Matrix with evidence
- Share insights with product epic owners

## Out of scope

- Paid ads campaigns
- Building features during interviews without backlog discipline
- National research firms (unless later justified)

## Dependencies

- `docs/04-business/Sales.md`, `Sales-Playbook.md`, `Personas.md`, `CustomerJourney.md`
- Access to local salon network

## Success metrics

- N interviews completed with written notes
- Top 5 pains validated by ≥3 independent sources
- At least one “do not build” decision recorded from evidence
