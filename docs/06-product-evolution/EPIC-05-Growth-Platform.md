# EPIC-05 — Growth Platform

## Status

Planned

## Goal

Create product capabilities that support acquisition, expansion and multi-module growth on top of a stable SaaS runtime.

## Why it matters

After Beauty excellence and tenant isolation, growth comes from onboarding leverage, expansion packs, and eventually new vertical modules sharing Core/Platform.

## Outcomes

- Faster self-serve or assisted activation hooks
- Clear expansion paths (extra seats, modules, add-ons)
- Architecture ready for a second vertical without rewriting Platform

## In scope

- Activation / empty-state guidance that drives first value
- Subscription/plan hooks aligned with Pricing (when billing is ready)
- API / integration surfaces that partners or future apps can use (incremental)
- Preparation work for next module (Sports/Clinic) — scaffolding only when justified
- Design System patterns reused across modules

## Out of scope

- Full second vertical MVP before Beauty + SaaS Runtime credibility
- National marketing campaigns (business epic)
- AI growth automation (EPIC-06)

## Dependencies

- EPIC-03 Beauty Excellence (credible core product)
- EPIC-04 SaaS Runtime (real multi-company)
- BUSINESS-EPIC-04 / 05 for onboarding and expansion motion
- ADR-001 Modular Architecture, ADR-003 Brand

## Success metrics

- Time-to-first-value for new companies
- Expansion revenue or seat growth (when monetized)
- Effort to spin a new module scaffold (engineering)
