# Customer Interview Playbook

**Status:** Living Document

**Owner:** Business Team

---

# Purpose

This playbook standardizes how Nexa conducts Customer Discovery interviews.

The objective is to understand customer operations rather than validate existing ideas.

Every interview should generate evidence for future product decisions.

---

# Before the Interview

Research the business.

Prepare:

- Business profile
- Current software
- Team size
- Main services

Review previous observations if available.

---

# During the Interview

Focus on understanding.

Avoid selling.

Use open-ended questions.

Examples:

- How do you organize your day?
- What consumes the most time?
- What causes mistakes?
- What frustrates you?

Observe more than you speak.

---

# After the Interview

Immediately:

- Update Field Observations.
- Create new Assumptions if necessary.
- Review Competitor Analysis.
- Schedule follow-up if needed.

Never rely on memory.
