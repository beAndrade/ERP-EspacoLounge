# Operations

`07-operations/` holds **operational execution** documentation.

Playbooks are step-by-step instructions for how someone performs a process in practice.

They must **consume** canonical business and product documentation. They must **not** redefine ICP, strategy, process, pricing, packaging, or brand identity.

Business strategy and official processes belong in [../04-business/](../04-business/README.md).
